About

Realm: · view on gnoweb ·

This marketplace trades any GRC721 collection whose owning realm registers an adapter satisfying gno.land/p/gnomarket/market.NFT — it never imports a specific collection's package. A seller approves this realm's address on their own collection (standard Approve or SetApprovalForAll) and lists — the token stays in their wallet until the moment it sells, atomically, in the same transaction as payment.
